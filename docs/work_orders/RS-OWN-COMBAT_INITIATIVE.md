# RS-OWN-COMBAT-INITIATIVE — Establish the Opening Turn Order

**DRAFT.** The director confirmed the direction on 2026-11-08. Primary 2 simulation, with 7 integration verification. [Product authority](../MASTER_GAME_ARCHITECTURE.md#04-director-clarification--2026-11-03)

Use the committed result of the noticed opening attack to establish the point at which turn-based play starts. Include the opening attacker and target if eligible, and include eligible characters near either one at that resolution. A character near both appears once. A character can receive a turn even if they did not notice the attack. Rank participants using individual speed, readiness and earned initiative advantage. Preserve the completed opening attack result, including a deflection, evasion or hit. [Onset contract](RS-OWN-COMBAT_RESOLUTION_ONSET.md)

The guard's choice to fight is separate from turn allocation. A guard fights when they discover someone they know is an outlaw, witness violence against an innocent civilian, or witness other violence that violates the laws they enforce. Use evidence available to that guard. A lock can constrain their movement and perception while leaving their opening turn intact. If the guard acts first against a known outlaw outside turn-based combat, resolve that attack under the existing onset rule.

The current world owner stores player village position in `VillageRecord`. `VillageBridge` reads authored resident placements through `village_supply` for display and interaction, then opens a separate `RoadCombatTrial` attachment for the combat test. The trial's `ClientSession.act` submits player commands and `EncounterActions._attack` resolves retaliation in the same action. Those paths supply neither a production combat roster nor independent participant turns.

Before marking the roster READY, define the spatial units, distance rule, and boundaries around both endpoints using authoritative positions. The area must include a character within its boundary even if a locked door blocks their route to the fight. Specify the speed and readiness inputs, how the opening attack affects readiness, the conditions and weight of earned initiative advantage, and deterministic tie-breaking. The production encounter owner must commit the opening order and execute the selected participant's turn. Guard behavior also needs enforceable local law, observation and recognition inputs. The existing social catalog associates residents with factions and countries. The knowledge path distinguishes sourced reports from committed events. These systems do not yet decide that a guard knows an individual to be an outlaw or witnessed unlawful violence. The [combat time and late entry draft](RS-OWN-COMBAT_WORLD_TIME.md) covers later scheduling requirements.

Verify a ranged attack with one character near the attacker only, one near the target only, one in both areas, and one outside both. Include the first three once each and leave the distant character outside. Include a nearby character behind a locked door without letting them cross it, and exclude defeated characters. Keep identical roster eligibility for a guard across three cases: recognizes the outlaw, witnesses unlawful violence by someone they do not recognize, and observes a stranger without evidence of an offense. The first two provide grounds for the guard to fight. Verify a faster participant acting first without surprise, earned initiative advantage changing the order, deterministic ties, and exact recovery and replay through the registered encounter. Cover deflection, evasion and hit transitions with one resolution each.
