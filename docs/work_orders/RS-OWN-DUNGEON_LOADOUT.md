Self-review. No independent, release or other-device gate applies to this prototype.

The seeded stone room grants three fixed starter choices. Short sword deals 2 basic damage and its pommel deals 1 and staggers. Spear deals 2 basic damage and its precision thrust deals 3. Hammer deals 1 basic damage and its crushing blow deals 2 and staggers. Each skill is usable once per encounter and remains spent across switching. Melee still requires cardinal adjacency and a committed first attack. `equip:<id>` uses the destination weapon's authored duration, one game second for each starter. It rotates the turn order once in combat, with no attack or retaliation bundled into that command. In exploration it advances the same duration. Unknown IDs, same weapon, wrong turn, wrong weapon skill and spent skill leave time and state unchanged. Ordinary non-dungeon combat keeps its defaults.
