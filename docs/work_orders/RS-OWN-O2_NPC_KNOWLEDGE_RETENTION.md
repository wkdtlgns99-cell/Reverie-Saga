## Decisions and constraints

- Director answer on initial visibility: only the directly addressed NPC knows the utterance at first. Over game time, other residents of the same city can learn it as a sourced rumor. It remains heard speech rather than an established world fact. Public and job-authorized facts use separate rules.
- Director answer on interpretation: keep the ordinary speech input. The NPC interprets the words in context and decides whether to repeat them. A player's implied expectation of privacy is part of the speech to interpret and may be misunderstood. The accepted decision remains a subjective judgment, with the original speaker and witness identified.
- Director answer on retention: retain 32 recent exchanges per NPC and up to 8 older salient spoken memories, with importance scores. Speech kept for longer still represents what was heard. The bounded observation is the merchant's actual supply delivery.
- The selected policy must apply to the actual player and NPC paths, including before a provider request. For any new retention profile, protect prior save pins and bytes, preserve active speech and idempotent retry, and test branch and checkpoint behavior. Keep the separate O3 two-NPC quest proof and commercial API payment outside this slice.

## Implementation contract

`python -m app.client --world-knowledge` starts the authored O1 gameplay with the new pinned knowledge policy and isolated `tmp/world-knowledge-play/game.sqlite`. `--npc-ai` opts into the existing natural language box and one external response per turn under the existing key and budget policy. Without a valid accepted reply no judgment is recorded. The heard exchange is still saved if generation fails, and previously committed rumors still advance with game time. Earlier profiles and their command, state and context bytes/pins remain unchanged.

The same `world.conversation` owner adds a versioned reply judgment, component/delta/patch variant and retention in its existing COMMIT. A judgment contains an integer importance 0–3 (ordinary, noticeable, personally meaningful, exceptional) and an optional exact contiguous quote of the delivered utterance, at most 160 Unicode code points. Empty quote means the NPC keeps it to themselves. The provider considers the addressed NPC's disposition, situation, activity and standing. Player and NPC text are untrusted game speech. Validate exact fields, range, types and source substring before acceptance. Never infer the existence of an objective event from the judgment, and never generate text inside simulation or replay. This profile uses current situational inputs and fixed disposition. Persistent mood, trust and indirect rumor chains are later contracts.

On a new speech append, keep that NPC's newest 32 exchanges regardless of score and the 8 best older positive-score records. Rank the older records by descending importance, then newest first for ties, and preserve chronological storage order. Other residents' retained records are unaffected by this append. Latest pending speech is never retired. Bound total by the existing world NPC catalog and existing record maximum. Nothing in core retrieval reads retired rows from replay. The actual merchant delivery observation comes from the existing authoritative `SupplyState.resolved_tick` and admission-bound merchant role. It is exposed to that merchant while in their city, survives conversation retirement and replay compaction, and never derives from a player's claim.

For an accepted repeat quote, other residents of the same city hear it in NPC ID order (excluding its original listener), one recipient per 10 game seconds after the reply is complete. At `reply_end + 9` nobody else knows it, at `reply_end + 10` the first recipient does, and the last recipient learns at `reply_end + 30` in a four-resident city. No wall clock, visit count, provider callback or multi-hop propagation. Derive eligibility solely from the active saved branch, clock and local roster. The projection emits only the authorized source quote, speech ID, original player actor, original listener ID/name, place and heard/learned ticks. Source retirement removes eligibility. The prompt receives up to two eligible rumors, newest first, and at most six own exchanges within the existing 4,096-byte own-memory budget. Public and role-bound facts retain their current projection. An assessed reply is bound to the same active speech, attachment and rebuilt context hash as ordinary replies.
